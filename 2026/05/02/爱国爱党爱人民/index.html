Comrades and friends

Today is a very important and solemn day in the history of the CPC and the Chinese nation. We are gathered here to celebrate the centennial of the founding of the CPC, look back on the glorious course of the CPC’s centennial struggle, and look forward to the bright future of the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation

First of all, on behalf of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China, I would like to extend warm festival congratulations to all Member of the Communist Party of China!

Here, on behalf of the Party and the people, I solemnly declare that through the continuous struggle of the entire Party and the people of all ethnic groups in the country, we have achieved the first centenary goal, built a moderately prosperous society in all respects on the land of China, historically solved the problem of absolute poverty, and are vigorously moving towards the second centenary goal of building a socialist modernized strong country in all respects. This is the great honor of the Chinese nation! This is the great honor of the Chinese people! This is the great honor of the CPC!

Comrades and friends!

The Chinese nation is a great nation in the world, with a civilization history of over 5000 years and an indelible contribution to the progress of human civilization. After the Opium War in 1840, China gradually became a semi colonial and semi feudal society. The country suffered humiliation, the people suffered hardship, and civilization suffered dust. The Chinese nation suffered unprecedented disasters. Since then, achieving the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation has become the greatest dream of the Chinese people and the Chinese nation.

In order to save the nation from danger, the Chinese people rose up in rebellion, and people with lofty ideals ran and shouted. The Taiping Heavenly Kingdom Movement, the Wuxu Reform, the Boxer Rebellion, and the Xinhai Revolution emerged one after another. Various national salvation plans were introduced in turn, but all ended in failure. China urgently needs new ideas to guide the national salvation movement and new organizations to unite revolutionary forces.

The October Revolution brought Marxism Leninism to China with the sound of a cannon. In the great awakening of the Chinese people and the Chinese nation, and in the close combination of Marxism Leninism and the Chinese labor movement, the CPC came into being. The emergence of the Communist Party in China was a groundbreaking event that profoundly changed the direction and process of the development of the Chinese nation after modern times, the future and destiny of the Chinese people and the Chinese nation, and the trend and pattern of world development.

Once the CPC was born, it established its original mission of seeking happiness for the Chinese people and rejuvenation for the Chinese nation. All the struggles, sacrifices and innovations that the CPC has united and led the Chinese people over the past 100 years can be summed up as one theme: to achieve the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.

——In order to realize the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation, the CPC united and led the Chinese people to fight hard and indomitable, and created the great achievements of the new democratic revolution. We have gone through the Northern Expedition War, Land Revolution War, Anti Japanese War, and Liberation War, opposing armed counter revolution with armed revolution, overthrowing the three great mountains of imperialism, feudalism, and bureaucratic capitalism, and establishing the People’s Republic of China where the people are the masters of the country, achieving national independence and people’s liberation. The victory of the New Democratic Revolution completely ended the history of the semi colonial and semi feudal society in old China, completely ended the scattered situation in old China, completely abolished the unequal treaties imposed on China by foreign powers and all the privileges of imperialism in China, and created fundamental social conditions for achieving the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation. The CPC and the Chinese people solemnly declared to the world with their brave and tenacious struggle that the Chinese people have stood up and the era of the Chinese nation being slaughtered and bullied is gone forever!

——In order to realize the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation, the CPC has united and led the Chinese people to rely on their own efforts and work hard to become stronger, creating great achievements in socialist revolution and construction. We have carried out the socialist revolution, eliminated the feudal exploitation and oppression system that has lasted for thousands of years in China, established the basic socialist system, promoted socialist construction, defeated the subversion, destruction, and armed provocation of imperialism and hegemonism, achieved the most extensive and profound social change in the history of the Chinese nation, and achieved a great leap forward in socialist society for the impoverished and populous Eastern powers, It has laid the fundamental political prerequisite and institutional foundation for achieving the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation. The CPC and the Chinese people solemnly declare to the world with their brave and tenacious struggle that the Chinese people are not only good at destroying the old world, but also good at building a new world. Only socialism can save China, and only socialism can develop China!

——In order to realize the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation, the CPC has united and led the Chinese people, emancipated their minds and forged ahead with determination, creating great achievements in reform, opening up and socialist modernization. We have achieved a profound and significant turning point in the history of the Communist Party of China since the founding of the People’s Republic of China. We have established the Party’s basic line in the primary stage of socialism, steadfastly promoted reform and opening up, overcame risks and challenges from all aspects, created, adhered to, defended, and developed socialism with Chinese characteristics, and achieved a transition from a highly centralized planned economy system to a vibrant socialist market economy system The historic transformation from closed and semi closed to all-round opening has achieved a historic breakthrough from relatively backward productivity to the second largest economy in the world. It has achieved a historic leap in people’s lives from insufficient food and clothing to an overall well-off society and towards a comprehensive well-off society, providing a new dynamic institutional guarantee and material conditions for rapid development to achieve the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation. The CPC and the Chinese people solemnly declared to the world with their brave and tenacious struggle that reform and opening up is the key to determine the future and destiny of contemporary China, and China has caught up with the times with great strides!

——In order to realize the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation, the CPC has united and led the Chinese people to be confident, self reliant, honest and innovative. It has taken charge of great struggles, great projects, great undertakings and great dreams, and created great achievements of socialism with Chinese characteristics in the new era. Since the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China, socialism with Chinese characteristics has entered a new era. We adhere to and strengthen the comprehensive leadership of the Party, coordinate the overall layout of the “Five in One” and the strategic layout of the “Four Comprehensives”, adhere to and improve the socialist system with Chinese characteristics, promote the modernization of the national governance system and capacity, adhere to governing the Party according to regulations, form a relatively complete system of Party regulations, and overcome a series of major risks and challenges, Realizing the first centenary goal and clarifying the strategic arrangements for achieving the second centenary goal, the Party and the state have achieved historic achievements and undergone historic changes, providing a more comprehensive institutional guarantee, a more solid material foundation, and a more proactive spiritual force for achieving the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation. The CPC and the Chinese people solemnly declared to the world with their brave and tenacious struggle that the Chinese nation has ushered in a great leap from standing up, getting rich to becoming strong, and the realization of the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation has entered an irreversible historical process!
